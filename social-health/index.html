      How-To: Meta Business Suite

      Step-by-step for every task you'll need to do — bookmark this

      Access: Go to business.facebook.com → log in with the business account. You'll land on the Home dashboard. Use the left sidebar to navigate between tools.

      ✍ Publishing a Post

      1. Click "Create post" (green button, top-right of any page)
      2. Select which page to post from (HALT, Hermetic Labs, or The Exchange) using the dropdown
      3. Paste your caption from the cadence document
      4. Click the photo/video icon to attach the campaign image
      5. Add hashtags at the bottom of the caption (copy from the Hashtag Intelligence section)
      6. To post now: click "Publish"
      7. To schedule: click the dropdown arrow next to Publish → "Schedule" → pick date/time
      8. To save as draft: click dropdown → "Save as draft"

      Tip: Meta only lets you schedule 30 days out. For posts further out, save them as drafts — you can find them later under Content → Drafts.

      📷 Setting a Cover Photo

      1. Go to the Facebook page directly (click the page name in Business Suite sidebar)
      2. Hover over the cover photo area → click "Edit cover photo"
      3. Upload the image (recommended: 820×312 px for desktop, 640×360 for mobile)
      4. Drag to reposition → click "Save"

      Files: Cover images are in the Social Media Package folder — use "windows banner.png" for HALT, "Hermetic Labs Logo Banner.png" for HL.

      🔗 Setting a Custom Page URL

      1. In Business Suite, go to Settings (gear icon, bottom-left)
      2. Select the page you want to edit
      3. Click "Page setup" or "General"
      4. Find "Username" → click "Edit"
      5. Type your desired username (e.g., HALTtriage, HermeticLabs)
      6. If available, click "Save"

      Note: Pages need 1+ follower and must be at least a few days old. If the option is greyed out, wait or try from the Facebook mobile app.

      📅 Publishing Drafts

      1. In Business Suite, click "Content" in the left sidebar
      2. Click the "Drafts" tab at the top
      3. Find the draft you want to publish
      4. Click "Edit post" to review the caption and image
      5. Make any changes → click "Publish" or "Schedule"

      Current state: There are 10+ drafts waiting under the HALT page, all from May 27. Start publishing these.

      📊 Checking Insights

      1. Click "Insights" in the left sidebar
      2. At the top, make sure you're looking at the right page (use the page selector dropdown)
      3. Overview: Shows reach, engagement, followers at a glance
      4. Content: Shows individual post performance (reach, clicks, reactions)
      5. Audience: Shows who your followers are (age, gender, location)
      6. Set date range to "Last 28 days" for weekly reporting
      7. Click "Export data" (top-right) to download as CSV/XLSX for the weekly report

      📶 Connecting Instagram

      1. Go to Settings → "Linked accounts" or "Instagram"
      2. Click "Connect account"
      3. Log in with the Instagram credentials
      4. Authorize Meta Business Suite access
      5. Once connected, when creating posts you'll see a toggle to "Share to Instagram"

      Important: Instagram must be a Professional/Business account (not Personal) to connect. Switch in IG Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account.

      How-To: LinkedIn

      Company page management — task by task

      Access: Go to linkedin.com → log in → click your profile icon (top-right) → under "Manage," click "Hermetic Labs LLC" to switch to the company page admin view.

      ✍ Publishing a Post

      1. Make sure you're in the company page admin view (not your personal profile)
      2. Click "Start a post" at the top of the page
      3. Paste your caption (shorter than Meta — LinkedIn rewards concise, professional text)
      4. Attach an image using the photo icon
      5. Add 3 hashtags max (more looks spammy on LinkedIn)
      6. Click "Post" to publish immediately
      7. To schedule: click the clock icon next to Post → pick date/time

      LinkedIn tone: More professional than Instagram/Facebook. Lead with insights, data, or thought leadership. "We built X because Y" — not "Check out our cool app!"

      🛠 Updating Company Specialties

      1. Go to the company page → click "Edit Page" (pencil icon near the top)
      2. Scroll to "Specialties"
      3. Remove: AR, VR, Mixed Reality (not relevant to current strategy)
      4. Add: Healthcare Technology, Digital Health, Humanitarian Technology, Field Medical Systems, Open Source, Emergency Response Technology
      5. Click "Save"

      👤 Building Connections (Follower Growth)

      1. Use the "Invite connections" button on the company page (Admin Tools)
      2. Search for people by title: "Digital Health," "Humanitarian Technology," "NGO IT"
      3. Invite 20–30 per week (LinkedIn limits invites)
      4. Comment on posts from WHO, MSF, ICRC, UNICEF — be insightful, not promotional
      5. Share relevant industry articles with company page commentary
      6. Join LinkedIn Groups: "Digital Health," "Humanitarian Innovation," "Global Health Technology"

      Key: LinkedIn organic reach comes from comments on others' posts more than your own publishing. Spend 15 min/day engaging with the humanitarian tech community.

      📊 Checking Analytics

      1. Go to company page → click "Analytics" in the top nav
      2. Visitors: Who's viewing the page (industry, company size, job function)
      3. Followers: Growth over time, demographics
      4. Content: Individual post performance (impressions, clicks, engagement rate)
      5. Use these numbers for the weekly report — LinkedIn's analytics are better than Meta's for B2B
      6. Click "Export" to download data as CSV

      📄 Creating a Document Post (Carousel)

      1. Click "Start a post" → click the "Document" icon (looks like a page)
      2. Upload a PDF (each page becomes a slide people swipe through)
      3. Add a title for the document
      4. Write a caption that hooks people into swiping ("Swipe to see how HALT works →")
      5. Document posts get 3x more engagement than text-only on LinkedIn

      Idea: Turn the "42 Languages" logos into a PDF carousel — one logo per page. Upload as a document post. Instant engagement magnet.
